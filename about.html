About Our Clinic

Zia Homeopathic Clinic is a homeopathic clinic based in Wah Cantt. We provide consultation options including in-clinic appointments, video consultations, and home visits where available.

Visitors can enquire about appointments, services, and medicine availability. Our focus is on clear communication and helping people get the information they need to make informed decisions about their enquiries.

The clinic is located on Darbar-e-Kareemi Road near Taji Hotel, Gulshan e Raza, G.T Road, Wah Cantt, and is open daily from 4:00 PM to 8:00 PM.

About Dr. Zia Toheed

Dr. Zia Toheed DHMS, RHMP

Dr. Zia Toheed is a homeopathic physician at Zia Homeopathic Clinic in Wah Cantt. He is qualified as DHMS and registered under RHMP.

At the clinic he sees patients for a wide range of everyday health concerns, with a focus on attentive one-to-one consultation and clear guidance.

Appointments are available in person, by video call, or as a home visit where suitable. For timings, availability or medicine enquiries, please contact the clinic on WhatsApp.
